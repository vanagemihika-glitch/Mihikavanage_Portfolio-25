Branding, Packaging

Gusto Gelato

A playful, modern brand manual for Gusto Gelato, featuring a cohesive visual identity with pastel tones, clean line art, and flavour-inspired packaging.

Branding, Strategy

Sadhna Re-branding

This project is a complete re-branding of Sadhna, a women-led handicraft NGO in Rajasthan. It introduces a new logo, color palette, and packaging including a bag, box, tag, wrapping paper, and visiting card

Illustration, Mural Design

Mural for a Restaurant

A vibrant mural celebrating Japanese culture, symbolizing happiness, strength, and perseverance. The mural conveys the importance of nourishment, resilience, and cultural heritage, blending Japanese elements with uplifting themes

Sweet Loops
